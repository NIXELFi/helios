## Analyst interpretation (post-hoc)

The automated scoring above flags 11 "BROKEN" and 11 "SUSPICIOUS" parameters,
but most are **scoring artifacts, not physics bugs**. The expectation labels
were assigned naively — a single shape per parameter, independent of how the
*other* parameters are simultaneously held fixed.  Below is a re-classification
based on inspecting each result table.

### Genuine bugs (Severity: HIGH)

1. **`intake_junction_loss_coef` is a no-op under `JunctionKind::Stagnation`.**
   All seven sweep values produce **bit-identical** IMEP/VE/EGT — the
   parameter passes through the SDM26 config but only takes effect in the
   `Characteristic` junction code path (see
   `crates/engine-sim/src/model/sdm26.rs:447` →
   `bcs/junction_characteristic.rs:538-539`). The Stagnation junction
   (`JunctionCV`) has no loss-coefficient field. Because SDM26's default
   and the bundled config both use Stagnation, every UI/optimization
   knob-twist on `intake_junction_loss_coef` is wasted. The schema entry
   should either be hidden when Stagnation is selected, or wired into
   `JunctionCV` as well.

### Scoring artifacts (BROKEN is misleading; physics is sane)

These show up as "BROKEN" only because the simple monotone/peaked label
doesn't match what the simulator actually does — and what the simulator does
is physically *correct* when you account for the fixed surrounding state.

2. **`bore` and `stroke` (MonoUp expected; observed: peak then collapse).**
   Sweeping bore from 50→120 mm at fixed cam timing, fixed 20 mm restrictor,
   fixed 50° burn, fixed valve sizes, etc. inflates V_d ~5.7× without
   improving filling — VE collapses (0.835 → 0.380 at 6000 RPM) and at
   12000 RPM × 120 mm the engine actually runs at negative BMEP (FMEP > IMEP,
   `brake_torque_nm = 0`). This is the correct response of a *fixed-setup*
   600cc engine asked to behave as a 4-liter. Re-scoring as **SANE
   (correct collapse signature)**.

3. **`runner_diameter_out` at 6000 RPM (MonoUp expected; observed: flat
   peak).** frac_range = 0.016 (1.6%). The "downward at 6000" is a barely-
   above-noise acoustic detuning from the runner-out diameter at this RPM.
   Monotone-up at 9000 and 12000 RPM matches expectation. Re-scoring as
   **SANE (small acoustic detuning at low RPM, expected response elsewhere)**.

4. **`combustion_duration` and `ignition_delay` (MonoDown expected; observed:
   peak).** Both have an interior peak. This is because **spark_advance is
   held fixed at 25° BTDC throughout the sweep**. With a fixed spark and a
   sweeping burn duration / ignition delay, the burn-CENTER (where 50% mass
   is consumed) shifts, and there is an interior optimum. If spark were
   re-MBT-optimized per duration, the expected monotone shape would emerge.
   Re-scoring as **SANE conditional on fixed-spark sweep**.

5. **`afr_target` (Peaked expected; observed: monotone-down across
   [11.5, 15]).** Brake power rises monotonically as AFR goes from 15 toward
   11.5, so the rich-power peak is *at or below* the schema's `suggested_min`
   of 11.5. Real engines often peak around 12-13 AFR; the simulator appears
   to peak below the schema range (or there's no rich quenching modelled).
   Worth a follow-up — if the peak is really below ~11.5 the schema range
   should be widened, or a charge-cooling effect added. Severity: **LOW
   to MEDIUM** (it's not wrong, just the suggested range hides the peak).

6. **`t_wall_cylinder` (MonoUp expected; observed: MonoDown).** Hotter
   cylinder walls reduce IMEP because they reduce VE (intake charge picks
   up heat from walls and loses density). The heat-loss-reduction effect
   exists but is weaker than the charge-heating effect at this scale.
   This is **plausibly correct** for a 1D solver that lumps charge-side
   wall heat into the cylinder wall T (no separate runner-wall thermal
   reservoir during intake stroke). Worth noting but not broken. Re-scoring
   as **SANE (weak ~3% effect with reasonable mechanism)**.

7. **`exhaust_valve_diameter` (MonoUp expected; observed: ~flat, slight
   VE dip).** VE falls 0.760→0.755 across the range (0.8%) while IMEP rises
   slightly (11.04→11.15, +1.0%). The metric I chose (`ve_atm`) is the wrong
   primary for this parameter — larger exhaust valve improves *scavenging*
   and *trapped-mixture quality*, which raises IMEP but can leave VE
   essentially unchanged. Re-scoring as **SANE under correct metric**.

8. **`intake_valve_open_angle` and `intake_valve_close_angle` (Peaked
   expected; observed: peak-at-boundary).** Both *do* show a peak at
   moderately-late-IVO and at IVC ≈ 605°. The verdict was BROKEN only
   because at 12000 RPM the peak sits at the upper edge of the swept
   range (380° IVO / 620° IVC) and my "must be strictly interior" check
   fired. Re-scoring as **SANE; expand schema range to capture the peak
   at high RPM**.

### Genuinely soft / numerical findings (Severity: LOW)

9. **`cfl` drifts IMEP by ~1-3% across [0.3, 0.95].** A perfectly grid-
   converged solver would be CFL-invariant. ~1-3% drift suggests there's
   some residual numerical dissipation that scales with timestep. The
   workspace default `cfl=0.85` puts the solver near the higher-dissipation
   end of that band. Consider running production sweeps at cfl≤0.5 for
   cleaner numerics; or examining whether the slope-limiter (`limiter`)
   is contributing. Not a bug — just a grid-convergence quality note.

10. **Cell-count knobs (`runner_n_cells`, `plenum_n_cells`, `primary_n_cells`,
    `secondary_n_cells`) are all <1% frac_range — well-converged numerically.
    The collector_n_cells / wall_t suspicious flags trigger at ~3%, which is
    on the edge — could be acoustic-resolution sensitivity. Acceptable.

11. **`wiebe_a` flagged Weak with frac_range up to ~10% at 12000 RPM —
    that's actually larger than I expected. Inspection of the data shows
    the IMEP rises monotonically with `wiebe_a` (more complete burn at
    higher `a`), which is physically correct. Re-scoring as **SANE; just
    a larger effect than the "Weak" expectation anticipated**.

### Interesting physical observations

- **Runner length tuning at 9000 RPM**: VE peaks at L = 0.50 m (top of the
  schema range) with VE = 0.665, vs 0.629 at L = 0.10 m — a 5.7%
  improvement. At 12000 RPM the same length gives VE = 0.569, vs 0.530
  at L = 0.10 m. The peak-VE length monotonically increases with the
  reciprocal of RPM, consistent with acoustic wavelength scaling. A
  quarter-wave estimate `f = a/(4L)` with a ≈ 340 m/s gives ~170 Hz at
  L = 0.5 m, which corresponds to engine-event frequency at ~10200 RPM
  (n_per_cycle = 1 intake event per crank rev per cylinder × half-cycle).
  That's ~10% off the observed-peak 9000 RPM — well within the precision
  of a 1D wave-tuning estimate (Helmholtz/end-correction effects can shift
  this by ~10-15%). **Verdict: acoustic tuning works as expected.**

- **Restrictor mass flow** at 12000 RPM goes from 3.10e-4 kg/cycle at 15 mm
  to 3.98e-4 kg/cycle at 25 mm — that's a 28% mass-flow increase for a
  178% area increase. Sub-linear → consistent with mass flow being
  partially choke-limited, the unchoked regime contributing a sub-linear
  area-scaling factor. No discontinuity; no choke-cliff because the
  schema's `suggested_min=15mm` is already well above the cliff edge
  (which would appear below ~10mm for this engine at 12000 RPM).

- **Drivetrain efficiency** is **exactly linear** as expected: per-row
  `wheel_kW / (brake_kW * eta) = 1.0000` across all 21 rows. Confirms
  the drivetrain is a pure post-engine multiplier.

- **Mass conservation** is *excellent*: peak `|nonconservation|` across all
  1239 runs is ~5e-18, i.e. machine epsilon — none of the parameters
  perturb conservation. No drift even under extreme sweeps. Solver
  integrity is solid.

- **Compression ratio**: at 6000 RPM, IMEP rises 10.0 → 11.7 bar across
  cr = 8 → 16. The Otto-cycle prediction (γ=1.35) is η = 0.485 → 0.626,
  a 29% relative improvement. The simulator delivers a 17% IMEP increase.
  Less than ideal Otto — consistent with heat-loss + finite-burn effects
  damping the theoretical gain. Reasonable.

- **Spark advance** is the textbook MBT peak: IMEP at 6000 RPM rises from
  9.83 at SA=5° to a max ~11.4 around SA=15-20° and falls back to ~10.5
  at SA=40°. Clean MBT bell. **Spark advance is one of the cleanest
  validators in the whole set.**

### Concrete next-step suggestions (no fixes performed)

- Wire `intake_junction_loss_coef` into `JunctionCV` (the Stagnation
  junction), or remove it from the schema for the Stagnation case.
- Consider widening `afr_target` schema range below 11.5 to expose the
  rich-power peak.
- Widen `intake_valve_close_angle` schema range above 620° (some race
  engines run IVCs at 630-640°) to capture the high-RPM tuning peak fully.
- Document the CFL grid-convergence drift (~1-3% IMEP across [0.3, 0.95]).
  Consider tightening the default to cfl ≤ 0.5 for production work.
- If `t_wall_cylinder` is intended to *only* represent cylinder-wall heat
  flux during the closed-cycle, decouple it from any intake-stroke charge-
  heating path — currently the sign of its IMEP effect suggests a coupling.
